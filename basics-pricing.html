What Decides the Cost of a Business Website

Think of it like building a shop. A small counter and a full showroom both count as a "shop", but the work and cost are very different. For a website, the main factors are these:

  • Number of pages. A simple 5-page site (Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact) takes less work than a 20-page site with many service and location pages.
  • Features. A WhatsApp button is simple. Online booking, payments, or an admin panel to update content yourself takes much more work.
  • Design level. A clean standard layout costs less than a fully custom design built around your brand.
  • Content. Who writes the text and who provides photos? Content that's ready on day one saves time and money.
  • Speed of delivery. Faster timelines often need more priority from the developer.
  • Support after launch. Some quotes include a few weeks of support. Others stop at delivery.

Costs People Forget

The price on the quote isn't always the full cost of owning a website. Ask about these before you agree:

  • Domain name. Your web address (like yourbusiness.in) is renewed every year. Check current prices with the registrar.
  • Hosting. The space where your site lives. Plans vary, so ask what's included and what the renewal cost is.
  • Photos and content. Professional photos, a logo or written content may cost extra if you don't have them ready.
  • Extra revisions. Many quotes include a fixed number of change rounds. Beyond that, changes can be charged.
  • Payment gateway charges. If you accept online payments, the payment provider usually charges a fee per transaction.
  • Updates and maintenance. Prices, timings and photos change. Decide who will update them and at what cost.
  • Promotion. A website needs visibility. Google Business Profile, local listings or ads are separate efforts.
  • GST. Ask whether the quoted price includes it.

At WEBORA, for example, domain and hosting are billed at actual cost in your name, and the packages include two rounds of changes. Whichever provider you choose, make sure these details are written down.

Mistakes to Avoid When Budgeting

1. Choosing only on price

A very low quote can mean missing features, no support, or paid extras later. Compare what's included, not just the total.

2. Not asking who owns the domain and files

Your domain should be registered in your name, and you should receive your website files after delivery. If you don't own them, you can get stuck later.

3. Paying everything upfront

A split payment, such as part at the start and part on delivery, keeps both sides committed. WEBORA uses a 50% advance and 50% on delivery.

4. Leaving the scope vague

Write down the number of pages, features, revision rounds and delivery date. Vague scope leads to arguments and extra charges.

5. Buying features you won't use

If you don't take online payments today, you may not need a payment gateway yet. Start with what your customers actually need and add features later.

6. Expecting a website to bring leads by itself

A website supports your business, but results depend on your offer, local demand, visibility and how fast you reply to enquiries. If you're still deciding whether you need one, read why a small business needs a website.

Questions to Ask Before You Pay

  1. What exactly is included: pages, features, design, content and support?
  2. Is the domain registered in my name? Who pays for hosting, and what is the renewal cost?
  3. How many rounds of changes are included?
  4. What is the delivery timeline, and what do you need from me to keep to it?
  5. Will my site work well on mobile phones, and can I see a demo?
  6. Do I get all the files and login details after delivery?
  7. What support is included after launch, and for how long?
  8. Does the quote include GST and all charges?

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the average cost of a small business website in Maharashtra?

There isn't one fixed average, because it depends on pages, features and support. As a reference, WEBORA's packages currently start at ₹12,999. Always compare quotes by what's included.

Can I get a website for under ₹10,000?

It's possible for a very simple single-page site. WEBORA's landing page, for example, is listed from ₹8,999. Check what's included, such as mobile design, domain, hosting and support, before you decide.

Are domain and hosting included in the price?

It depends on the provider, so ask. At WEBORA, domain and hosting are billed at actual cost and registered in your name.

Do I need to pay every month after the website is built?

Not always. You'll usually pay yearly for the domain and hosting. Monthly maintenance is optional, but useful if you want regular updates and monitoring.

How long does it take to build a business website?

It depends on the number of pages and how quickly you share content. WEBORA's packages list delivery between 7 and 20 business days.

Will a more expensive website get me more customers?

Not automatically. Results depend on your offer, local demand, Google visibility and your follow-up. Spend on what your customers need, not on features that look impressive.
