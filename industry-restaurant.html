How Restaurants & Cafes Are Getting More Table Bookings with a Simple Website

Digital menu, WhatsApp takeaway orders and online table reservation — practical ways a restaurant website increases dine-in and delivery orders.

By the Webora Team Published: 29 Jul 2026 5 min read

On this page

  • A digital menu people can actually browse
  • WhatsApp ordering for takeaway and delivery
  • Simple online table reservations
  • Photos that do justice to your food
  • Location and parking info upfront
  • Reviews and ambience, not just the menu

A digital menu people can actually browse

A PDF menu that's hard to read on a phone loses customers before they've even decided what to order. A clean, mobile-friendly menu page lets people browse at their own pace and decide what they want before they even walk in.

WhatsApp ordering for takeaway and delivery

Instead of losing a cut to third-party delivery apps on every order, a direct WhatsApp ordering link keeps more of the sale with you, and customers already trust the platform they use every day.

Simple online table reservations

A short reservation form — party size, date, time, contact number — saves your staff from juggling phone calls during busy hours, and gives customers the confidence that their table is actually held.

Photos that do justice to your food

This is the one place where genuinely good photography pays off. A few well-lit photos of your best dishes do more to bring people in than any amount of written description.

Location and parking info upfront

First-time visitors often decide whether to come based on how easy you are to find and park near. Clear directions and parking notes remove hesitation before they've even left home.

Reviews and ambience, not just the menu

People choose a restaurant for the experience, not only the food. A short line about your ambience — outdoor seating, live music nights, family-friendly space — helps people picture themselves there.
