Why Your Current Website Is Slow on Mobile (And How It Is Costing You Customers)

Most local business websites take 4–6 seconds to load on mobile. Learn what a sub-1.2s website can do for ranking and inquiries.

A few seconds matters more than you'd think

Most visitors decide whether to stay on a page within the first few seconds. If your site takes 4–6 seconds to load, a large share of visitors have already left before they've seen anything you built.

Heavy themes are usually the culprit

Many local business sites are built on page builders loaded with plugins and features they barely use. All of that extra code has to load before a visitor sees your content, and it adds up fast.

Large, unoptimised images

A photo straight from a phone camera can be several megabytes. Compressed and properly sized, the same photo can load in a fraction of the time with no visible difference in quality.

Slow hosting quietly undermines everything else

Even a lean, well-built website will feel sluggish on cheap, overcrowded hosting. Reliable hosting is a foundational piece of speed, not something to cut corners on.

Speed affects your search ranking too

Search engines factor page speed into how they rank pages. Two similar local businesses competing for the same search terms can rank very differently based partly on how fast their sites load.

What sub-1.2 second loading actually feels like

A fast site feels almost instant — visitors barely notice the load at all, which keeps their attention on your content and your call-to-action instead of on a spinning loading icon.
